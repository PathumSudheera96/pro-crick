Agency Background

Pro-Crick is a cricket talent connection platform built with one clear purpose: to bridge the gap between talented cricketers and cricket clubs by creating meaningful, transparent and long-term partnerships.

Founded by cricket enthusiasts with a deep understanding of the game, Pro-Crick was developed through years of research, discussions and planning to identify the needs of both players and clubs within the modern cricket landscape.

Our initial focus is connecting talented Sri Lankan cricketers with cricket clubs across the United Kingdom. Sri Lanka has a proud cricketing heritage and continues to produce players with exceptional skills, passion and dedication. Pro-Crick aims to provide opportunities for:

•⁠  ⁠Professional cricketers seeking overseas opportunities
•⁠  ⁠Emerging and amateur cricketers looking to develop their careers
•⁠  ⁠Sri Lankan players already based in the United Kingdom seeking the right club environment

As Pro-Crick grows, our vision is to expand beyond Sri Lanka and create a global network connecting cricketers from different nations with clubs across the UK and international cricket communities.
